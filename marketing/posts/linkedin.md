I shipped a small Mac app this week: wallpap.

It replaces the still picture behind your desktop icons with a living scene. There's a koi pond, a snowy cabin, a record store at night, and a Santorini terrace with cats. You can click them, and they react.

I built it because my desktop is the thing I look at most, and it was the least considered part of my day. A few details I cared about:

- It stays out of the way. Clicks only reach the scene on bare desktop, scenes keep their main action clear of your widgets, and it pauses when you're away, keeping its last frame.
- It follows your day. Light tracks your real clock, live weather brings the rain, and the moon is in tonight's real phase.
- It helps a little. Calm turns the scene into a breathing guide. Water and breathe reminders appear inside the world, not as another notification.
- It plays along. Music Mode puts your Apple Music or Spotify track on the record sleeve or the rooftop projector, and the room moves with the beat.

All 12 scenes are free. Pro is $5, once, with no subscription.

If you try it, I'd love to hear which scene you keep: wallpap.live
