## Description (≤500 chars)

wallpap puts calm, living scenes behind your desktop icons: a koi pond, a snowy cabin, a record store at night, cats on a Santorini terrace. Click them and they react. Light follows your real clock and the weather. Music Mode puts your Apple Music or Spotify track into the scene, and Calm turns it into a breathing guide. It's a tiny menu-bar app that pauses when you're away. All 12 scenes are free; Pro is $5 once.

## Maker's first comment

Hi Product Hunt, I'm the maker of wallpap.

My desktop is the thing I see most every day, and it was always a still photo I'd stopped noticing. I wanted something closer to a window: quiet, alive, and a little bit playful when I reach for it.

So wallpap runs a real scene behind your icons. Each one is hand-tuned to stay calm while you work:

- **Touch.** Click the pond and the koi come for the food. Toss the cats a toy, drop bamboo for the pandas, strike a singing bowl. Clicks only reach the scene on bare desktop, so Finder works exactly as before.
- **Your day.** Light follows your clock, with a slow day cycle if you like. Live weather brings rain when it rains outside, and the moon is in tonight's phase.
- **Music Mode (Pro).** Your Apple Music or Spotify track lands on the record sleeve, the rooftop projector or the ramen-alley radio. Beat Sync analyses sound on your Mac and never reroutes it.
- **Calm (Pro).** Breathing happens inside the scene, with box, 4·6 or 4-7-8 patterns. Gentle water and breathe reminders appear in-world, or as a small card when apps cover the desktop.
- **Light on energy.** It pauses when you're away or on battery and keeps its last frame until you click.

All 12 scenes are free in their default look. Pro is $5, once, and works on up to 3 Macs. New scenes arrive in the app without an update. Night Drive and more visualizers are next.

I'd love to know which scene you keep, and what you'd want to see on your desktop next.
