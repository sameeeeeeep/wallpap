How it works

It lives where your wallpaper lives.

  1. 1

    Behind your icons

    A tiny menu-bar app places the scene just above your wallpaper — under your icons, on every Space and every display. Your files stay exactly where they are.

  2. 2

    Click the desktop to play

    Show your desktop and the scene comes alive under your cursor: feed the koi, strike a bowl, toss the yarn. Clicks inside your other apps never leak through.

  3. 3

    Rests while you work

    When you're not looking at the desktop it softly blurs and stops rendering entirely — about 0.5 energy impact — and wakes the moment your cursor comes back.

Small, kind details

A companion, not a screensaver.

  • Weather & time aware

    Real local weather — rain rings on the pond, snow on the terrace — and light that moves from dawn to dusk to fireflies.

  • Gentle wellbeing nudges

    A sip of water, a shoulder roll, a breath — told in-world by a blooming lotus or a thirsty cat, then gone.

  • Calm · Breathe

    Everything slows into a 4-second inhale, 6-second exhale. The koi circle, the candles breathe, the cats curl up together.

  • Soundscapes

    Rain, ocean, fireplace, a stream, or brown, pink and white noise — light, looping, and generated on your Mac.

  • Music Mode

    Night Café shows what's playing in Apple Music or Spotify — album art on the sleeve, the title on the chalkboard.

  • Energy-smart

    Frame-capped while you play, fully paused while you work. Optional pause on battery. Your fans won't notice it's there.

  • Private by design

    No account. No tracking. Location is optional and only used to ask for your weather. Everything else stays on your Mac.

  • Every display, every Space

    One scene follows you across monitors and desktops, sized and lit for each screen.

Questions

Good to know.

Will it drain my battery?

No. wallpap only draws while you're actually looking at your desktop, and frame-caps itself while it does. The rest of the time it blurs softly and stops rendering completely — around 0.5 energy impact in Activity Monitor. You can also choose Pause on Battery or Pause Animation from the menu bar.

What happens to my privacy?

Nothing leaves your Mac. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking. Location is optional: if you allow it, wallpap uses it only to fetch your local weather from Open-Meteo. If you don't, it uses your time zone's city. Music Mode reads the current track locally and never sends it anywhere.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. It's a ~1 MB download, and it works across multiple displays and every Space.

macOS says it “can't be opened.” Is it safe?

Yes — it's tiny, open source, and runs entirely on your Mac. wallpap isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch needs one extra step: open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. We're working on notarization.

How do I quit and get my normal wallpaper back?

Click the 〰 wave in your menu bar and choose Quit. Your wallpaper comes straight back, because wallpap never changes it. It just floats a scene above it. To stop it starting with your Mac, untick Open at Login in the same menu.

Will it get in the way of my desktop icons and files?

No. Your icons stay on top and stay clickable, and drag-and-drop works as normal. wallpap only reacts when you click bare desktop, and clicks inside your other apps never reach it.

Yes. It's $9 once, with every future scene and update included — no subscription. Pro is opening soon; join the list and we'll let you know the moment it's available.
