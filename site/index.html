Questions

Before you download.

How do I install it?

Download wallpap.dmg, open it and drag wallpap into Applications, then open it. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so there are no security warnings to click through. The wave 〰 then lives in your menu bar.

Will it drain my battery?

Animation uses energy while it is running. wallpap limits its frame rate and pauses rendering when the desktop is inactive. You can also choose Pause on Battery or Pause Animation from the menu bar.

What happens to my privacy?

No account is required. Weather requests send location coordinates to Open-Meteo; the app uses an approximate time-zone location unless you enable precise location. Sun and moon calculations run locally. Music and beat analysis also run on your Mac. Optional AI companions reflect activity from Claude Code and Codex.

Which scenes are free?

The three Nature scenes — Koi Pond, Touch Grass and Santorini Cats — are free forever, with every interaction. Pro unlocks the Cozy Rooms (Night Café, Snowy Cabin, Record Store), City Nights (Speakeasy, Rooftop, Ramen Alley), the Indian Train and Singing Bowls, plus everything we make next.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. It works across multiple displays and every Space.

How do I quit and get my normal wallpaper back?

Click the 〰 wave in your menu bar and choose Quit. Your wallpaper comes straight back, because wallpap never changes it — it just floats a scene above it. To stop it starting with your Mac, untick Open at Login in the same menu.

Will it get in the way of my desktop icons and files?

No. Your icons stay on top and stay clickable, and drag-and-drop works as normal. wallpap only reacts when you click bare desktop, and clicks inside your other apps never reach it.

Is Pro really a one-time payment?

Pro is planned as a $5 one-time purchase. It isn’t available to buy yet. You can try all the scenes in the browser while the three free scenes are available in the Mac app.
