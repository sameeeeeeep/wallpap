Questions

Good to know.

How do I install it?

Download wallpap.dmg, open it and drag wallpap into Applications. On first launch macOS will stop it, because it isn't notarized by Apple yet: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. The wave 〰 then lives in your menu bar.

Will it drain my battery?

No. wallpap only draws while you're actually looking at your desktop, and frame-caps itself while it does. The rest of the time it blurs softly and stops rendering completely. You can also choose Pause on Battery or Pause Animation from the menu bar.

What happens to my privacy?

Nothing leaves your Mac. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking. Location is optional: if you allow it, wallpap uses it only to fetch your local weather from Open-Meteo. Music Mode reads the current track locally. Beat Sync listens to your Mac's audio on-device to find the beat — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. AI companions only notice that Claude Code or Codex is running; they never read your code.

Which scenes are free?

The three Nature scenes — Koi Pond, Touch Grass and Santorini Cats — are free forever, with every interaction. Pro unlocks the Cozy Rooms (Night Café, Snowy Cabin, Record Store), City Nights (Speakeasy, Rooftop, Ramen Alley), the Night Train and Singing Bowls, plus everything we make next.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. It works across multiple displays and every Space.

How do I quit and get my normal wallpaper back?

Click the 〰 wave in your menu bar and choose Quit. Your wallpaper comes straight back, because wallpap never changes it — it just floats a scene above it. To stop it starting with your Mac, untick Open at Login in the same menu.

Will it get in the way of my desktop icons and files?

No. Your icons stay on top and stay clickable, and drag-and-drop works as normal. wallpap only reacts when you click bare desktop, and clicks inside your other apps never reach it.

Is Pro really a one-time payment?

Yes. It's $9 once, with every future scene and update included — no subscription. Pro is coming soon; join the list and we'll let you know the moment it's available.
