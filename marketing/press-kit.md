# wallpap — press kit

**Living wallpapers for Mac.** wallpap is a small macOS menu-bar app that puts calm, animated scenes behind your desktop icons. You can click the scenes and they react. They follow your real time of day and weather, can show the music you're playing, and can breathe with you.

## One-liner
Your desktop, alive: calm, interactive scenes behind your Mac's icons.

## Short description (50 words)
wallpap replaces the still picture behind your desktop icons with a living scene: a koi pond, a snowy cabin, a record store at night, cats on a Santorini terrace. Click and they react. Light follows your clock, rain follows your weather, and your music shows up in the room. Every scene is free.

## Long description (120 words)
wallpap runs a real, animated scene behind your icons and widgets on every display and every Space. There are twelve scenes today: Koi Pond, Touch Grass, Santorini Cats, Corner Café, Snowy Cabin, Record Store, Speakeasy, Rooftop, Ramen Alley, Train Journey, Singing Bowls and Cymatics, a music visualizer where sand forms Chladni patterns. You can feed the koi, toss the cats a toy, give the pandas bamboo and pet the dog. Clicks only reach the scene on bare desktop, so the Mac works exactly as before. Pro ($5, once) adds every skin, Music Mode (your Apple Music or Spotify track in the scene, moving with the beat), Calm (breathing inside the scene), water and breathe reminders, soundscapes, live weather and AI companions. A paused scene keeps its last frame and uses almost no energy. New scenes arrive without an app update.

## Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| Platform | macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple Silicon & Intel · multiple displays, every Space |
| Distribution | Direct download (DMG), signed & notarized by Apple |
| Price | Free: every scene in its default look, every interaction. **Pro: $5 one-time**, license for up to 3 Macs (checkout by Dodo Payments) |
| Account | None required |
| Privacy | Live weather sends approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo. Sun/moon, music and beat analysis run on-device. Audio is never rerouted. |
| Scenes | 12 at launch. In progress: Night Drive (endless road), more visualizers, light nature scenes |
| Built with | Native Swift host (one WKWebView per display, below the desktop icons) + scenes as self-contained HTML (Canvas2D / WebGL) |
