# Feature bullets (App-Store style)

Use these in store listings, directory submissions (Setapp-style sites, MacUpdate, AlternativeTo), press emails and image alt text.

**Subtitle (≤30):** Living wallpapers you can touch

**Promo line (≤170):** Calm, animated scenes behind your desktop icons. Feed the koi, play your music in the room, breathe with the scene. Every scene free; Pro $5 once.

## Highlights

- **Living scenes.** Koi Pond, Touch Grass, Santorini Cats, Corner Café, Snowy Cabin, Record Store, Speakeasy, Rooftop, Ramen Alley, Train Journey, Singing Bowls and Cymatics.
- **Reach in.** Feed the koi, toss the cats a toy, give the pandas bamboo, pet the dog, strike a singing bowl.
- **Your day, your weather.** Light follows your clock. Rain arrives when it rains outside. The moon is in tonight's phase.
- **Music Mode.** Your Apple Music or Spotify track appears on the record sleeve, the projector or the radio, and the scene moves with the beat. *Pro*
- **Calm.** Breathing happens inside the scene: koi ripples, gusts through the grass, string lights. Box, 4·6 or 4-7-8. *Pro*
- **Gentle reminders.** Water and breathe nudges appear in the world, or as a small card over your apps. *Pro*
- **Soundscapes.** Rain, ocean, fireplace, stream, and brown, pink or white noise. *Pro*
- **Stays out of the way.** Icons stay clickable, and scenes keep their action clear of your widgets.
- **Light on energy.** It pauses when you're away or on battery and keeps a crisp last frame. Click to resume.
- **One menu-bar panel.** Scenes, time of day, weather, sound, music, calm and energy.
- **Always growing.** New scenes arrive without an app update.
- **Simple pricing.** Every scene is free. Pro is $5 once, for up to 3 Macs. No account, no subscription.
